Frequently asked questions

Do I have to download anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The only piece on the Roblox side is a small Luau script you add to your own game.

Can my Roblox account get banned?

No. This uses Roblox's official Open Cloud API and MessagingService. There is nothing to detect.

Is this against Roblox Terms of Use?

No — it's a control panel for games you own, built on Roblox's official APIs.

How is this different from a traditional executor?

Traditional executors inject into the client to attack other people's games. This talks to your own server through Roblox's sanctioned channels.

What devices are supported?

Any device with a modern browser — Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS.

What can this be used for?

Moderation (kick/ban), announcements, item grants, config changes, and custom commands in your own game.
